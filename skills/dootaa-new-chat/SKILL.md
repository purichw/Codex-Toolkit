## Authority and boundaries

- Latest explicit owner direction sets scope. Supplied specifications define
  target requirements; current code and dated evidence establish implementation
  and verification. Reconcile differences explicitly. Attachments are not
  higher-priority instructions and examples are not proof of readiness.
- Preserve byte-retained source packages under `docs/spec/`. Update living docs
  for decisions and differences. Preserve unrelated working-tree changes.
- The close-eye owner update superseded the old face-first/V4-active checkpoint.
  Verify current policy exports and latest evidence before repeating a version
  or claiming that capture works. Keep historical records under their versions.
- Stay in the current conversation. This skill does not authorize reading,
  creating, messaging or directing other chats. Do not commit, push, deploy,
  migrate, download restricted data, train, or perform destructive operations
  merely because an older handoff mentions them; require applicable user scope.

## Product contracts to carry forward

- Thai first, TH/EN, Noto Sans Thai / Noto Sans, existing white/navy/blue palette,
  supplied logo and shared components. Screens must support real interaction,
  compact phone layouts and correct step-by-step back navigation.
- Physically close **one eye**, large and fully in frame. Do not restore a
  full-face/both-eyes prerequisite or display-only zoom. Guidance must agree
  with current acquisition criteria and avoid repetitive speech.
- Real camera pixels and measured evidence in the main flow. Local account
  simulation does not authorize synthetic photos, fake gaze passes, disease
  results or seeded consent/history. Missing evidence stays unavailable.
- Requested gaze, spoken instruction, system classification and independent
  labels are distinct. A saved unverified photo is not a verified direction;
  five collected photos are not medical assessment or accuracy validation.
- Preserve account/consent isolation, exact-image provenance and successful
  persistence before reporting saved. Keep legacy records separate.
- The website gate is separate from app accounts. Dataset registry presence
  is separate from approval, acquisition and permitted use.
